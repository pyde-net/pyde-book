# Pyde Tokenomics

This document defines the current economic separation between the three Pyde environments.

## 1. Tier 1

Tier 1 uses the participating jurisdiction's sovereign digital currency as its native monetary asset.

Its issuance, redemption, regulatory treatment, and monetary policy remain under the jurisdiction's authority.

PYDE is not the sovereign settlement asset.

## 2. Tier 2

Tier 2 charges protocol fees for settlement and related coordination services.

Fees are denominated in the applicable sovereign currency of the participating settlement environment rather than forcing sovereign activity to use PYDE.

The current Tier 2 fee allocation is:

| Allocation        | Share |
| ----------------- | ----: |
| Validators        |   60% |
| Protocol treasury |   40% |

No sovereign currency is burned by the Tier 2 fee mechanism.

The validator share compensates the open validator set for consensus, foreign exchange aggregation, and settlement infrastructure.

The treasury share funds protocol development, security, infrastructure, ecosystem operations, and approved network expenses.

## 3. Protocol Treasury

The protocol treasury is network controlled economic state.

It is not a corporate bank account.

Independent service providers can earn commercial revenue by providing development, security, infrastructure, research, maintenance, or other approved services to the protocol under explicit contracts.

Protocol funds and company revenue therefore remain separate.

## 4. Tier 2 Validator Alignment

PYDE can be used by Tier 2 validators through the public Tier 3 validator economy.

This makes PYDE a validator alignment and public network security asset rather than the sovereign settlement currency of Tier 2.

## 5. Tier 3

Tier 3 is the permissionless public economic environment.

PYDE is the public protocol asset and can be used for public network functions such as execution fees, validator participation, and other protocol defined purposes.

The exact Tier 3 fee and supply parameters are implementation specific and should be documented from the current public network specification rather than inferred from the Tier 2 model.

## 6. Design Principles

### Monetary neutrality

Tier 2 does not require participating sovereigns to adopt PYDE as their settlement currency.

### Economic separation

Sovereign monetary state, protocol economic state, and company commercial revenue are distinct systems.

### Open validator economics

Tier 2 permits independent validators subject to the network's requirements.

### Auditability

Economic allocations should be derivable from canonical protocol state and published rules.
